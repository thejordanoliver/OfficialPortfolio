About me

Digital Media BA

I'm Jordan Oliver, a creative thinker and web designer passionate about innovation, accessibility, and user engagement. I graduated from the University of Central Florida in Spring 2025. I specialize in UI/UX design, front-end development, and digital experiences that merge functionality with creativity.

My background spans visual design, digital marketing, and API development, with experience in tools like Figma, React Native, Framer, and PostgreSQL. I've worked on projects ranging from sports app development to event-planning platforms and alternative gaming controllers. I focus on crafting intuitive, visually compelling designs that improve user interactions.

Beyond design, I have a deep appreciation for sports, gaming, and sneaker culture. I enjoy exploring how technology can elevate those experiences, whether I'm refining an interface, coding an interactive feature, or brainstorming new solutions.
